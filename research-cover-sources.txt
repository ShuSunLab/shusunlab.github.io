Scientific integrity
The cover contains 32 original figure excerpts and one original shared legend from nine papers. Figures were extracted from their public PDF versions and embedded without changes to scientific content. Original axes, legends, labels, annotations, colors, and numerical values are retained. All figures are proportionally sized. They represent distinct studies rather than a demonstrated common experimental pipeline.

https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.11255v1
